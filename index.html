    Words you'll see

    Volatility
    How wildly the price jumps around. Higher means more rollercoaster.
    Drawdown
    The biggest fall from a peak. It shows how much pain past holders felt.
    Moving average
    The average price over recent days. Price above it usually means an uptrend.
    RSI
    A 0–100 gauge. Above 70 often means the stock ran up too fast; below 30, it fell too fast.
